# Finura Client Presentation & Demo Runbook (2–4 Minutes)

This runbook guides presenters and clients through an end-to-end walkthrough of Finura.

---

## Presentation Sequence

### 1. Landing Page Overview (0:00 – 0:40)
- Navigate to `http://localhost:5173/`.
- **Key Talking Points**:
  - Show heading: *"Take Control of Your Financial Future"*.
  - Point out core value proposition: unified accounts, budgets, goals, investments, and AI assistance.
  - Review 7 feature cards (Money, Budgets, Goals, Investments, Wealth, Credit, AI).
  - Walk through How-It-Works workflow (Connect -> Track -> Budget -> Plan).
  - Emphasize Security Section (isolated data tenancy, encrypted fields, JWT authentication).
  - Showcase interactive Dashboard Preview (privacy-friendly, responsive).
  - Click **"Explore Demo"** or **"Get Started"**.

### 2. Registration & "Getting Started" Onboarding (0:40 – 1:15)
- Enter Signup page or click "Explore with Instant Demo Account" on Login.
- Demonstrate the **7-Step Onboarding Modal**:
  - Step 1: Welcome to Finura
  - Step 2: Add Account (Click "Open Accounts Page" -> shows Accounts module)
  - Step 3: Record Income (Shows Transactions entry)
  - Step 4: Add Expenses (Shows Expense categorization)
  - Step 5: Set Monthly Budget (Shows Budgets configuration)
  - Step 6: Create Financial Goal (Shows Goal creation)
  - Step 7: Explore Finura AI (Shows AI Copilot)
  - Complete the tour or show Skip functionality.

### 3. Core Money Management & Dashboard (1:15 – 2:00)
- **Overview Dashboard**:
  - Point out Total Net Worth, Monthly Income, Monthly Expenses, and Savings Rate KPI cards.
  - View real-time Cash Flow chart and Account balances.
- **Money & Accounts**:
  - Demonstrate HDFC Salary Account, SBI Everyday Checking, Cash, and ICICI Credit Card.
- **Transactions**:
  - View income credits (Salary, Freelance) and expense outlays (Rent, Groceries, Utilities).

### 4. Budgets & Goals (2:00 – 2:30)
- **Smart Budgets**:
  - Show Food budget (₹12,000), Transport (₹5,000), and Shopping (₹8,000) with visual progress meters.
- **Financial Goals**:
  - Review Emergency Fund Target (₹180,000 target, ₹112,000 saved) and Home Upgrade Fund.

### 5. Investments, Credit & AI Copilot (2:30 – 3:15)
- **Investments & Wealth**:
  - Review asset allocation pie chart, equity holdings, and SIP schedules.
- **Credit Solutions**:
  - Review credit score (780+), active credit cards, and repayment dates.
- **Finura AI Assistant**:
  - Ask: *"What is my current monthly savings rate and how can I optimize it?"*
  - Review context-aware recommendations generated by the AI engine.

### 6. Reports, Settings & Logout (3:15 – 3:45)
- **Exporting**:
  - Navigate to Reports -> Click "Export PDF" / "Export CSV" to demonstrate report generation.
- **Settings**:
  - Show base currency toggle (USD/INR/EUR), risk profile preferences, and **"Restart Onboarding Tour"** button.
  - Click "Restart Onboarding Tour" to demonstrate that clients can re-trigger the walkthrough anytime.
- **Logout**:
  - Click Logout to confirm clean session termination and return to Landing Page.
