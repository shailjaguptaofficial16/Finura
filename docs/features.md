# Finura Feature Catalog & Functional Specification

## 1. Landing Page
- **Hero Banner**: "Take Control of Your Financial Future" with direct CTAs for registration and instant demo evaluation.
- **Feature Matrix**: Covers Money Management, Smart Budgets, Financial Goals, Investment Tracking, Wealth Overview, Credit Management, and Finura AI.
- **Workflow Journey**: 4-step progressive explanation from connecting accounts to long-term wealth intelligence.
- **Security Assurance**: Explains JWT authentication, data isolation, encryption standards, and zero third-party selling.
- **Dashboard Preview**: Responsive mockup demonstrating the internal dashboard UI with privacy-safe figures.

## 2. "Getting Started" Onboarding Flow
- **Step 1 — Welcome**: Orient the client to their personal financial workspace.
- **Step 2 — Add Account**: Direct link to add bank, cash, or card ledger accounts.
- **Step 3 — Record Income**: Prompt to log monthly earnings and salary streams.
- **Step 4 — Add Expenses**: Guide to track routine outflows and categorize transactions.
- **Step 5 — Set Monthly Budget**: Define category limits for spending discipline.
- **Step 6 — Create Financial Goal**: Establish target milestones for emergency reserves and assets.
- **Step 7 — Explore Finura AI**: Launch AI assistant for automated spending audits and surplus tips.
- **Key UX Features**: Step indicators (Step X of 7), Skip option, Back/Forward navigation, keyboard navigation (Escape/Enter), persistent completion state, and restart option in Settings.

## 3. Financial Management Modules
- **Overview Dashboard**: High-level KPI meters (Net Worth, Monthly Income, Expenses, Savings Rate) and cash flow trends.
- **Money & Accounts**: Bank accounts, checking accounts, cash wallets, credit cards with balances and account details.
- **Transactions Ledger**: Filterable history with income/expense classifications, dates, categories, and payment methods.
- **Smart Budgets**: Real-time spending versus budget allocation meters with color-coded warning thresholds.
- **Financial Planning & Goals**: Target timelines, saved amounts, progress percentages, and automated completion projections.
- **Investment Tracking**: Real-time portfolio valuation, asset distribution charts, and SIP monitoring.
- **Credit Solutions**: Card balance tracking, utilization ratios, credit score monitoring, and repayment calendars.
- **Finura AI Assistant**: Conversational financial intelligence engine answering queries, analyzing leaks, and providing actionable optimization advice.
- **Reports & Export**: One-click statement generation supporting PDF documents and CSV data downloads.
